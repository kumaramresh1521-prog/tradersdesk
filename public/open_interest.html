⚡ Understanding Open Interest Walls

The strike with the highest Call Open Interest acts as the strongest overhead resistance wall, where writers defend premiums. Conversely, the strike with highest Put Open Interest creates the primary floor support.

⚖️ Put-Call Ratio (PCR) Interpretation

A PCR > 1.2 indicates aggressive put writing relative to calls, signaling bullish institutional sentiment. A PCR < 0.8 signals heavy call writing and cautious or bearish bias. Range 0.8–1.2 reflects balanced market equilibrium.

🎯 Max Pain Strike Significance

The Max Pain strike is the theoretical price point where option buyers suffer maximum financial loss upon contract expiry, maximizing cumulative profits for institutional options sellers.
